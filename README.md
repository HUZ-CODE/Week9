# BookHub - Your Premier Online Bookstore

A modern, responsive online bookstore application built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Features

- **Book Catalog**: Browse through a curated collection of 9 featured books
- **Advanced Filtering**: Filter books by:
  - Categories (Fiction, Non-Fiction, Mystery, Sci-Fi, Romance, Biography)
  - Price Range (Under $20, $20-$50, $50-$100, Over $100)
  - Ratings (3+, 4+, 5 Stars)
- **Search Functionality**: Search books by title, author, or category
- **Sorting Options**: Sort by:
  - Default order
  - Price (Low to High / High to Low)
  - Highest Rating
  - Newest First
- **Shopping Cart**: Add/remove books, adjust quantities, and view cart summary
- **Responsive Design**: Mobile-friendly interface that works on all devices
- **Notifications**: Toast notifications for user actions
- **Professional UI**: Modern gradient design with smooth animations

## How to Use

1. Open `book-shop.html` in your web browser
2. Browse the book catalog
3. Use filters and search to find specific books
4. Click "Add to Cart" to add books to your shopping cart
5. Click the shopping cart icon (top-right) to view your cart
6. Adjust quantities or remove items as needed
7. Click "Proceed to Checkout" to complete your purchase
